# Operator Hypertext retrieval rules

For areas represented in `hypertext/`, begin with `hypertext/index.md` and the linked project record before broadly searching status history or the repository. Follow references to source, configuration, logs, examples, and reports as needed to verify claims, provenance, chronology, or contradictions.

Search `reports/status.md` directly when Hypertext has no applicable entry, coverage is incomplete or conflicting, the requested fact or chronology is not represented, or historical reconstruction is required. Hypertext is a retrieval aid and semantic index; it does not replace primary evidence or the append-only journal.
