# Curator Hypertext rules

When creating or reconciling Hypertext records, preserve source provenance, scope, coverage, lifecycle, and epistemic classification. Do not silently resolve a conflict between a record and its source; retain it until primary evidence supports a resolution. Link claims to evidence and mark unsupported or unexamined areas as unknown or uncovered.

Do not represent sibling-worktree records as REConsole evidence. Keep separate targets (REConsole, Yi .55, and ZHENYI/SGK) explicit in record scope.
